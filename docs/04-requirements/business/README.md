# Business Requirements (BRD)

Business Requirements Documents state **business outcomes and their justification** — the "why" layer of the requirements chain. They contain goals, scope, stakeholders, constraints, and financial rationale; they never contain feature designs or system choices.

- Template: [TPL-0002](../../02-templates/TPL-0002-business-requirements-template.md)
- Naming: `BRD-NNNN-<short-title>.md` per [STD-0002](../../00-standards/STD-0002-naming-conventions.md)
- Approval: business sponsor + Head of Product ([GOV-0002](../../01-governance/GOV-0002-approval-workflow.md))
