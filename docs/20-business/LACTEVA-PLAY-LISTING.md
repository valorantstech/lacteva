# Google Play Listing

Everything the Play Console will ask for, prepared before the console exists
so that publishing is a filling-in exercise rather than a drafting one. The
console account, the upload keystore and the signing decision are owner-side
(**D-11**) and are not in this document.

**Nothing here may overclaim.** The copy below is checked by
`tools/brand/check_play.py`, which runs the marketing site's own claim
patterns — read out of `apps/marketing-site/src/app/claims.test.ts`, not
copied — over this file. A listing sits in a store nobody greps, which is
precisely why it needs the guard the website has.

## 1 · Assets

Both are generated by `python3 tools/brand/generate.py` and committed under
`tools/brand/play/`. They are regenerable, and they derive from the same
geometry as the launcher and the favicon, so the store shows the mark the
product actually has.

Since **LACTEVA-BRAND-004** the icon is the milk **can** with the drop knocked
out of its belly, and the feature graphic carries the **full lockup and the
tagline** — the can, the owner's traced LACTEVA letterforms and "Smart Dairy.
Stronger Tomorrow." — in cream on the dairy gradient.

| Asset | File | Spec applied |
|---|---|---|
| Hi-res icon | `tools/brand/play/icon-512.png` | 512 × 512, 32-bit PNG, **fully opaque**, 9 KB |
| Feature graphic | `tools/brand/play/feature-graphic-1024x500.png` | 1024 × 500, **RGB with no alpha channel**, 79 KB |

**Why the icon is full-bleed and opaque.** Play composites its own rounded
mask and drop shadow onto the store icon. The product's rounded field would
therefore be rounded twice, and the transparent corners left by that rounding
would show the store's own background through the gap. A full-bleed opaque
square is correct under either reading of the policy — it is a 32-bit PNG,
and it has no transparency — and `check_play.py` asserts the alpha channel is
255 everywhere rather than trusting that it is.

**The banner is now reproducible, and therefore checked.** Its wordmark used
to be drawn with cairo's toy font API against whatever `sans-serif` resolved
to on the build machine, so the committed PNG could only be taken on trust and
regenerating it elsewhere produced a diff. BRAND-004 replaced that with the
owner's traced outlines, which are committed data — so `check_play.py` now
regenerates BOTH assets and compares them pixel for pixel. A store showing a
logo the product does not have is a failure, not a cosmetic difference.

**Safe margins on the feature graphic.** Play crops this banner on some
surfaces and lays a play button over its centre on others. Everything that
carries meaning sits inside a central box inset by 104 px horizontally and
62 px vertically, which leaves roughly a tenth of the width and an eighth of
the height clear on every side.

**A screenshot set is still outstanding** — Play requires at least two phone
screenshots and this repository has no device-capture step. See DISCOVERED in
the LACTEVA-LAUNCH-001a completion report.

### Full description

<!-- listing:full -->
```
Lacteva is the field app for dairies that collect milk from farmers.

At the collection counter
Record a collection in one flow: identify the farmer, capture the weight,
capture fat, SNF and CLR, and let the platform price it against the rate card
that covers that centre. The farmer's parchi is issued by the platform with
its own slip number, and can be read out or copied at the counter.

Works without a signal
Collections captured with no connection are held in a durable queue on the
phone and sent when the network returns. The queue survives closing the app
and restarting the handset. The screen always says how many items are waiting,
so nobody has to guess whether the morning's work has reached the dairy.

For the round and the van
Delivery rounds and driver runs are in the same app: today's stops in route
order, what each household takes, and one tap to record what was delivered or
missed. Outcomes queue offline exactly as collections do.

For the household
A customer sign-in shows that household its own deliveries, this month's
quantity, what is owed and every invoice, and nothing belonging to anyone
else.

Built for the counter
Large targets and large type for a phone used one-handed, outdoors, sometimes
by someone wearing gloves. English, Hindi and Arabic, chosen from the person's
own account rather than from the handset. Money is shown exactly as the
platform calculated it — this app performs no pricing arithmetic of its own.

Lacteva is used by staff of a dairy that already has an account. It is not a
consumer marketplace and it does not sell milk.
```

## 4 · Data safety

**Derived from the code, not from memory.** Each answer below names what was
read.

### What the app is permitted to do

`apps/mobile/android/app/src/main/AndroidManifest.xml` declares exactly one
permission across the main, debug and profile manifests:

```
android.permission.INTERNET
```

There is no location, camera, microphone, contacts, storage, phone or
calendar permission. The app cannot collect those things because it cannot
reach them.

### What third parties are in the build

`apps/mobile/pubspec.yaml` lists `flutter`, `cupertino_icons`, `http`,
`path_provider` and `qr_flutter`. **There is no analytics SDK, no advertising
SDK and no crash-reporting SDK.** No third party receives anything.

### Where data goes

`apps/mobile/lib/main.dart` defines one base URL (`LACTEVA_API_URL`), and a
grep of `apps/mobile/lib/` finds no other host. Everything the app sends goes
to the dairy's own Lacteva platform.

### The answers

| Play question | Answer | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Does your app collect or share any of the required user data types? | **Yes — collected, not shared** | Below |
| Personal info › Email address | **Collected.** Used for account management (sign-in). Required. **Not shared.** | `centers.dart` `LoginScreen`, `api.dart` `login()` |
| Personal info › Name | **Collected.** The signed-in person's name comes from the platform for display. Required. Not shared. | `session.dart` `Session.fullName` |
| Personal info › User IDs | **Collected.** The platform's own user id, for app functionality. Not shared. | `session.dart` `Session.userId` |
| Is data encrypted in transit? | **Yes**, for any HTTPS deployment. The base URL is deployment-configured; the pilot's own configuration must use HTTPS. | `api.dart` uses `http` against `LACTEVA_API_URL` |
| Can users request data deletion? | **Via the dairy.** Accounts are administered by the dairy, not self-service. State this honestly on the form. | no in-app account deletion exists |
| Location | **Not collected** | no location permission, no plugin |
| Financial info | **Not collected from the user.** The app records the DAIRY's business figures — collection quantities, prices, settlements — on the dairy's behalf. It takes no payment and holds no card. | no payment SDK; `payments.dart` is read-only |
| Photos / videos / audio / files | **Not collected** | no camera, no storage permission |
| Contacts / calendar / SMS / call logs | **Not collected** | no permission |
| Device or other IDs | **Not collected.** The device identifier the sync engine sends is the literal string `mobile-device`, not a hardware or advertising id. | `main.dart` `deviceId ?? 'mobile-device'` |
| App activity / diagnostics / crash logs | **Not collected** | no analytics or crash SDK |

### Two facts worth stating plainly on the form

* **The session token is never written to disk.** `api.dart` holds it in a
  private field on the client instance and clears it on sign-out; nothing
  persists it. A stolen handset yields no stored credential.
* **The offline queue holds the dairy's business records, not the user's
  personal data.** `offline/queue.dart` writes queued collections and
  deliveries to the app's own private documents directory
  (`lacteva_sync_queue.json`) so a morning's work survives a restart. It is
  app-private storage, and it is transmitted only to the dairy's own platform.

**Push notifications are not wired in this build.** `push.dart`'s default
`PushTokenSource` is `NoPushConfigured`, whose `token()` returns null, so no
messaging token is obtained or sent. If a messaging vendor is added later,
this section and the data-safety form both change.

## 5 · Content rating

The questionnaire is answered in the console, but the answers are known:

* No violence, sexuality, profanity, gambling, drugs or horror.
* No user-generated content shared between users. Operators record business
  data that only their own dairy's staff can read.
* No user-to-user communication, no chat, no social features.
* No purchases in the app. Money the app shows is what the dairy owes a
  farmer or is owed by a household, calculated by the platform.
* Location is not shared, because it is not collected.
* **Target audience: adults**, specifically employees of a dairy business.
  This is not a children's app and must not be enrolled in Designed for
  Families.

Expected outcome: **Everyone / PEGI 3 / rated for all ages**, with no
interactive elements declared beyond "users interact" being answered *no*.
