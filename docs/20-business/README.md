# 20 — Business & Commercial

The **live** business documents: what Lacteva sells, to whom, on what terms, and
what a dairy must hand over before a pilot can start. These are working papers a
person acts from — an onboarding call, a pricing conversation, a go-live
decision — not records of work already done. Those are next door in
[21 — Milestones](../21-milestones/README.md).

They moved here from the repository root (LACTEVA-ARCH-002), where eighty-five
markdown files sat side by side and the nine that were still in use were
indistinguishable from the seventy-four that were history.

- Naming: descriptive `LACTEVA-<SUBJECT>.md`; these predate the `NNNN` numbering
  in [STD-0003](../00-standards/STD-0003-document-numbering.md) and keep the
  names their readers already know.
- Front matter, versioning and change logs follow
  [STD-0001](../00-standards/STD-0001-markdown-writing-standards.md) like every
  other governed document.
- The repository is the source of truth: where one of these disagrees with
  [ARCHITECTURE_BASELINE_V1](../../ARCHITECTURE_BASELINE_V1.md) or the code, the
  repository wins.

## Rules

- A document here is **current**. When it stops being something a person acts
  from, it moves to `21-milestones/` rather than rotting in place.
- Commercial terms that are not yet decided are marked **TO CONFIRM** rather
  than guessed — an invented price in a sales pack is worse than a blank one.
- No document here may invent a capability. The claims guards in the product
  suites exist because that mistake has been made before.
